One year after the first rally, Birmingham was back for the second national
**Stand Up for Science** day of action: the **Rally to Take Back Science** on
Saturday, March 7, 2026. Birmingham Indivisible was a partner organization.

The national theme: *Save Science, Protect Health, and Defend Democracy.*
