On March 7, 2025, Birmingham joined dozens of cities across the country for the
first national **Stand Up for Science** day of action.

About **500 people** gathered at Railroad Park, near Children's of Alabama. Many
were students and faculty from UAB. The day opened with an **"Ask a Scientist"
panel**, followed by the rally and march.

UAB neuroscientist **Dr. Kristina Visscher** spoke about proposed federal funding
cuts. PhD and MD-PhD trainees talked about what the cuts could mean for early-career
scientists and for UAB's medical research.

**Details**

- **When:** Friday, March 7, 2025, 12:00 to 3:00 PM
- **Where:** Railroad Park, 1600 1st Ave. S., Birmingham (Webb Memorial Gardens area)
- **Program:** "Ask a Scientist" panel, then rally and march
