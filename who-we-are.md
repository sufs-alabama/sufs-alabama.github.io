    <div class="navy-box" id="who">
      <p>We're researchers, students, healthcare workers, teachers, engineers, and neighbors
      who believe science belongs to everyone. Alabama is home to world-class research,
      from rocket propulsion to genomics to medicine, and that work keeps our communities
      healthy, employed, and curious.</p>
      <p><strong>We're standing up for science in Alabama, now and for generations to come.</strong></p>
    </div>
    <div class="navy-box" id="what" style="margin-top: 24px;">
      <h2>What We Do</h2>
      <p><strong>Defend research.</strong> Stable, independent funding for the science that powers Alabama's universities, hospitals, and industries.</p>
      <p><strong>Make science public.</strong> Trivia nights, talks, and meetups where anyone can ask questions and get real answers.</p>
      <p><strong>Show up together.</strong> Rallies and advocacy that put Alabama voices in the national conversation.</p>
    </div>

</section>
